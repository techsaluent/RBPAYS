Data Policy

What we collect, why, how long we keep it, and your rights.

Last updated: 7 September 2026

This Data Policy describes how REAL BROTHERS TECHNOLOGY SERVICES LLP ("TutiPays", "we") handles data on the TutiPays platform. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy. For account/data deletion, see Account & Data Deletion.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email, mobile number, username, role and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • KYC data: PAN, Aadhaar reference, business/shop proof and related documents you submit for verification.
  • Transaction data: the services you perform, amounts, references, wallet ledger entries and commission records.
  • Customer data you enter: details of an end-customer you assist (e.g. a beneficiary or a bill), processed by us on your behalf.
  • Technical data: device, IP address, login/session records and app usage needed for security and fraud prevention.

2. Why we use it

  • To provide the services, run your wallet and settle commission.
  • To verify identity (KYC) and meet RBI, PMLA, tax and other legal obligations.
  • To secure the platform, detect and prevent fraud and money laundering, and manage risk.
  • To provide support, resolve disputes and send service and (optional) marketing communications.

3. Consent

We process your data on the basis of your consent (given at sign-up and when you use a service), to perform our contract with you, and to comply with law. Where a service requires a customer's Aadhaar/biometric consent (e.g. AEPS, Aadhaar Pay), that consent must be obtained for each transaction; such biometric data is used only to authenticate that transaction and is never stored.

4. Sharing

We share data only as needed to deliver the services and meet the law: with RBI-authorised banks, payment system operators and networks (e.g. NPCI), aggregators and the specific service providers that process a transaction; with KYC/verification agencies; with tax and regulatory authorities; and with courts or law-enforcement on a lawful request. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose collected, and then delete or anonymise it. Financial and KYC records that we are legally required to keep (under RBI directions, the PMLA and tax laws) are retained for the prescribed period — typically 5–8 years — even after you delete your account, and are then deleted. See Account & Data Deletion for details.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored on access-controlled systems. Passwords are stored only as hashes, card numbers are masked, secrets and API keys are hashed/masked, and administrative access is restricted and audited. No system is perfectly secure, but we apply bank-grade controls appropriate to the data.

7. Your rights

  • Access & correction: view and update your profile in the panel, or ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: request deletion of your account and data — see Account & Data Deletion (subject to the legal-retention exception above).
  • Withdraw marketing consent: opt out of marketing messages at any time.
  • Grievance: raise a concern via Grievance Redressal.

8. Contact

For any data question, email support@tutipays.com.
