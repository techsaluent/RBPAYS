Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know to get started and grow.

Getting started

What is TutiPays?

TutiPays is a B2B fintech platform by REAL BROTHERS TECHNOLOGY SERVICES LLP that lets retailers and distributors offer digital financial and utility services — money transfer, AEPS, recharge, bill payments, payouts, travel, insurance and more — from a single wallet, and earn commission on every successful transaction.

Who can join TutiPays?

Any lawful retail or distribution business in India. You can join as a Retailer, Distributor or Master Distributor. Only Retailer accounts perform end-customer transactions; Distributors and Master Distributors build and manage their network.

How do I sign up?

Click Sign Up, create your account, and complete KYC by submitting your identity and business documents. Once approved, load your wallet and start transacting.

KYC

Why is KYC required?

KYC (Know Your Customer) is mandatory under RBI guidelines and anti-money-laundering law. It verifies your identity and business before you can transact. You submit documents such as PAN, Aadhaar, GST and business proof through the panel.

How long does KYC approval take?

KYC is typically reviewed within 1–2 business days. You'll see your status update in the panel from "Pending" to "Verified" once approved.

Wallet & money

How do I add money to my wallet?

You can top up your TutiPays wallet by depositing cash into a listed company bank account, by bank transfer/UPI, or through the in-panel payment gateway. For cash/bank deposits, submit the reference (UTR) in the panel and your wallet is credited once verified.

Is my wallet balance safe?

Your wallet reflects the prepaid funds you've loaded for availing services. Transactions are signature-verified and fully logged with a printable receipt, and balances are protected with bank-grade security.

What happens if a transaction fails?

If a transaction fails, the amount and charges are reversed to your wallet, usually automatically once the bank/operator confirms. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Commission

How does commission work?

Every successful transaction earns commission, which is distributed automatically across the retailer, distributor, master distributor and the company as per the applicable commission plan. Commission slabs are visible in your panel.

When is commission credited?

Commission is credited in real time on successful transactions. If a transaction is later reversed, the related commission is recovered.

Services & limits

What services can I offer?

DMT money transfer, AEPS & Micro ATM, mobile/DTH recharge, BBPS bill payments, payouts, UPI, Aadhaar Pay, Card Swipe, CMS, PAN card, wallet transfer, travel booking and insurance.

Are there transaction limits?

Yes. Limits are set per applicable RBI and operator guidelines and our risk policy. For example, Domestic Money Transfer has per-transaction and monthly per-remitter ceilings. Limits are enforced automatically in the panel.

Is TutiPays a bank?

No. TutiPays is a technology and distribution platform. Regulated services are delivered through RBI-authorised banks, payment system operators and their Business Correspondent / aggregator networks.

Support

How do I get help?

Email support@tutipays.com for support, or admin@tutipays.com for business queries. Complaints follow our Grievance Redressal process.
