About TutiPays

Empowering India's retailers with one platform for every digital service.

Who we are

TutiPays is a B2B financial technology platform operated by REAL BROTHERS TECHNOLOGY SERVICES LLP, a Limited Liability Partnership incorporated in India on 17 February 2024. We build the software rails that let neighbourhood retailers, distributors and master distributors deliver assisted digital financial and utility services to millions of customers across India — especially in semi-urban and rural areas where a trusted local shop is the front door to the digital economy.

What we do

Through a single wallet and one simple panel, our partners can offer:

  • Domestic Money Transfer (DMT) — IMPS/NEFT to any bank account
  • Aadhaar Enabled Payment System (AEPS) & Micro ATM cash withdrawal
  • Mobile & DTH recharge and BBPS bill payments
  • Payouts, UPI, Aadhaar Pay and Card Swipe (mPOS)
  • CMS cash collection, PAN card services, wallet transfers
  • Travel booking and insurance

Every successful transaction earns transparent, automatically-distributed commission across the retailer, distributor and master distributor network.

TutiPays is a technology and distribution platform. Regulated services are delivered in partnership with RBI-authorised banks, payment system operators and their Business Correspondent / aggregator networks. We are not a bank or an NBFC.

Our mission

To make financial services accessible, affordable and reliable at the last mile — turning every local shop into a full-service digital financial point, and helping our partners grow a sustainable income.
