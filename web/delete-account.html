Account & Data Deletion

Request deletion of your TutiPays account and associated personal data.

Last updated: 7 September 2026

This page explains how you can request deletion of your account and personal data on the TutiPays platform, operated by REAL BROTHERS TECHNOLOGY SERVICES LLP ("TutiPays", "we"). It applies to our website, the partner panel and the mobile app.

1. How to request deletion

You can request deletion of your account and data in any of these ways:

  • From the panel / app: log in and go to Profile → Delete my account, then confirm. Your request is recorded immediately and our team verifies and processes it as described below.
  • By email: alternatively, write from your registered email to support@tutipays.com with the subject "Delete my account", stating your registered mobile number.

Verification: Because TutiPays accounts hold a wallet and financial transaction history, we verify that a deletion request genuinely comes from the account holder before we act on it.

2. What gets deleted

Once your request is verified and any pre-conditions below are met, we delete or irreversibly anonymise the personal data linked to your account, including:

  • Your profile details — name, email, mobile number, username and login credentials.
  • KYC documents and identifiers you uploaded (subject to the legal-retention exception below).
  • Device bindings, saved beneficiaries and in-app notifications.
  • Marketing preferences and any support/marketing profile.

3. What we must retain (and why)

As a business facilitating regulated financial services, we are legally required to keep certain records even after account deletion. We retain the following for the period prescribed by law and then delete them:

  • Transaction and settlement records and the double-entry ledger, as required under RBI directions, the PMLA (anti-money-laundering) and tax laws — typically for 5–8 years from the transaction.
  • KYC records where retention is mandated by the PMLA / KYC Master Directions.
  • Records needed to resolve a pending dispute, chargeback or investigation, or to comply with a lawful request from a court, regulator or law-enforcement agency.

Retained records are access-restricted and used only for the legal purpose for which they are kept; they are not used for any other purpose after deletion.

4. Pre-conditions

  • Your wallet balance must be nil. Withdraw any remaining balance to your bank account before requesting deletion; undisputed balances can also be refunded to your registered bank account during processing, subject to verification and settlement of dues.
  • There must be no pending transaction, payout, dispute or recovery on the account.
  • Any dues payable by you to TutiPays must be cleared.

5. How long it takes

Verified requests are actioned within 30 days. Where a pending transaction, dispute or a statutory retention period applies, the corresponding data is deleted at the end of that period. We will confirm by email once your request has been processed.

6. Effect of deletion

Deletion is permanent. You will lose access to your account, history and any unused benefits, and you will need to register afresh (and complete KYC again) if you wish to use TutiPays in future.

7. Contact

For any question about account or data deletion, email support@tutipays.com. See also our Data Policy and Privacy Policy. Unresolved matters can be escalated via Grievance Redressal.
