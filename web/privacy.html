Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal and business information.

Last updated: 17 February 2024

This Privacy Policy explains how REAL BROTHERS TECHNOLOGY SERVICES LLP ("TutiPays", "we", "us"), operator of the TutiPays platform at tutipays.com, collects, uses, stores, shares and protects information about you. It is published in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and applicable data protection law in India.

1. Information We Collect

  • Identity & KYC data: name, photograph, date of birth, PAN, Aadhaar (as permitted), address, business/shop details, GST number and bank account details.
  • Contact data: mobile number, email address.
  • Transaction data: service used, amount, beneficiary details, operator, status, timestamps and references.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser information, and log data.
  • Customer data you enter: where you assist an end-customer (e.g. a DMT beneficiary or a bill), you may enter their details, which we process on your behalf.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and operate your account and wallet, and to verify your KYC.
  • To process, route and settle transactions through our banking and aggregator partners.
  • To calculate and distribute commission across your network.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, money laundering and misuse, and to meet legal and regulatory obligations.
  • To provide support, send service communications, and improve the Platform.

3. Legal Bases & Consent

We process your information based on your consent, the performance of our contract with you, our legitimate business interests, and to comply with legal and regulatory requirements (including RBI guidelines and PMLA). Aadhaar-based authentication, where used, is performed strictly through authorised channels with your explicit consent, and we do not store your Aadhaar number or biometric data beyond what is permitted.

4. How We Share Information

We share information only as necessary:

  • With RBI-authorised banks, payment system operators, aggregators and Business Correspondents to execute transactions.
  • With KYC and verification service providers.
  • With regulators, law enforcement and courts where required by law.
  • With service providers (e.g. hosting, communications) under confidentiality obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls, hashed credentials, signature-verified transactions and audit logging, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure; you are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active and thereafter for the period required to comply with legal, regulatory, tax and audit obligations (which for financial records is typically several years).

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and may withdraw consent. Some data must be retained to meet legal obligations. To exercise your rights, contact us at support@tutipays.com.

8. Cookies

The Platform uses essential cookies and local storage to keep you logged in and to operate the panel securely. Disabling them may impair functionality.

9. Children

The Platform is intended for businesses and persons aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time; the revised version will be posted here with an updated date.

11. Grievance Officer

For privacy questions or complaints, contact our Grievance Officer at admin@tutipays.com. See our Grievance Redressal page for details and timelines.
