Grievance Redressal

How to raise and escalate a complaint, and our commitment to resolve it.

Last updated: 17 February 2024

At REAL BROTHERS TECHNOLOGY SERVICES LLP (TutiPays), we are committed to fair, prompt and transparent handling of customer complaints. This page sets out how to raise a grievance and how we escalate and resolve it.

Level 1 — Customer Support

For any transaction issue, dispute or query, first contact our support team:

  • Email: support@tutipays.com
  • Please include your registered mobile/email, the transaction ID/reference, and a description of the issue.
  • Response time: acknowledgement within 24 hours; resolution typically within 5–7 working days, subject to confirmation from the underlying bank/operator.

Level 2 — Grievance Officer

If your complaint is not resolved at Level 1 within the stated time, or you are not satisfied with the resolution, you may escalate to our Grievance Officer:

Escalation to Banking / Network Partners

Certain regulated transactions (such as DMT, AEPS, Micro ATM and BBPS) are processed through RBI-authorised banks, payment system operators and networks (e.g. NPCI). Where a dispute pertains to such a transaction, it may be raised with, and resolved as per the timelines of, the relevant bank/operator and network dispute mechanism. We will assist you in routing such complaints.

If a complaint relating to a digital payment is not resolved within the timelines prescribed by the Reserve Bank of India, you may also approach the RBI Ombudsman under the Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme through the RBI's official portal.

Information to Provide

  • Registered name, mobile number and email
  • Transaction ID / reference and date
  • Service (DMT, AEPS, recharge, etc.), amount and beneficiary details
  • A clear description of the issue and any supporting screenshots/receipts

We value your trust and will work sincerely to resolve every genuine grievance.
