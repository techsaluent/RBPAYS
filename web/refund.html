Refund & Cancellation Policy

How reversals, refunds and disputes are handled.

Last updated: 17 February 2024

This policy explains how refunds, reversals and cancellations work for transactions carried out on the TutiPays platform operated by REAL BROTHERS TECHNOLOGY SERVICES LLP.

Please note: Most services on TutiPays are real-time financial transactions (money transfer, recharge, bill payment, AEPS withdrawals). Once a transaction is successful, it generally cannot be cancelled or reversed by TutiPays, because the funds have already reached the beneficiary, operator or biller.

1. Failed Transactions

If a transaction fails or does not complete, the transaction amount and applicable charges are reversed to your TutiPays wallet. This reversal is usually automatic once the underlying bank / operator confirms the failure. In some cases the status may remain "pending" until the operator confirms; the amount is settled or reversed once the final status is received.

2. Wallet Top-ups

Amounts you load into your TutiPays wallet are meant for availing services and are non-refundable as a matter of routine. Any unutilised, undisputed wallet balance may be refunded to your registered bank account on account closure, subject to KYC verification and deduction of any dues.

3. Service-specific Handling

ServiceRefund / Reversal
Money Transfer (DMT)Successful transfers cannot be recalled. Failed/returned transfers are auto-reversed once the bank confirms, typically within a few minutes to a few working days.
Recharge & DTHSuccessful recharges are non-refundable. Failed recharges are reversed to your wallet.
BBPS Bill PaymentsSuccessful bill payments are non-refundable. Failed payments are reversed as per BBPS timelines.
AEPS / Micro ATMCash withdrawal disputes are handled through the bank / NPCI dispute process.
Travel & InsuranceCancellations and refunds follow the terms of the respective operator / insurer and may attract cancellation charges.

4. Raising a Dispute

If an amount is debited but the service was not delivered, raise a dispute within 7 days of the transaction by contacting support@tutipays.com with the transaction reference/ID. We will investigate with the relevant bank / operator and communicate the outcome. Disputes are resolved subject to confirmation from the underlying partner and applicable network (e.g. NPCI) timelines.

5. Chargeback & Fraud

Fraudulent, disputed or reversed customer payments (e.g. card chargebacks) may be debited from your wallet. You are responsible for verifying customer identity where required.

6. Commission on Refunds

Commission is credited only on successful transactions. If a transaction is later reversed, any commission credited for it will be recovered.

7. Contact

For refund or dispute assistance, email support@tutipays.com. Unresolved matters may be escalated via our Grievance Redressal process.
